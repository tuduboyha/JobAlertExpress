How to Get a Government Job?

Landing a government job is not only about submitting an application — you also need to prepare for the written exam, skill test or interview that follows, depending on the recruiting organisation. Along with your qualification, many posts also require a valid age limit, category-specific relaxation, and specific documents, so always read the official notification carefully before applying.
